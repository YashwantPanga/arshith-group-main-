FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers regarding eligibility, mentorship structure, stipends, and certificate verification.

General & Eligibility

Who is eligible to apply?

Pre-final year students, final year undergraduates, postgraduates, and recent graduates in Engineering, MCA, BCA, Design, and Business streams are eligible to apply.

Is the internship Remote or On-site?

We offer Remote, Hybrid, and On-site options depending on your location and domain track. Daily standups are conducted via Microsoft Teams and Discord.

Certification & Career Benefits

Do I get a certificate and LOR?

Yes! Every intern receives an official, digitally verifiable Certificate of Completion. Exceptional performers receive a personalized Executive LOR and PPO interview fast-tracking.

Selection Process & Timeline

What is the selection process timeline?

After submitting your application form, our talent acquisition team evaluates profiles within 24-48 hours, followed by a short technical assessment task and interview.
